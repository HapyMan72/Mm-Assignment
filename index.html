Why does your bottle’s next step matter?

Every packaging item has a lifecycle. When you dispose of your Coca-Cola bottle or can in a dedicated recycling bin, you allow materials to be repurposed into new packaging, reducing waste and helping keep plastic out of oceans.

3 Quick Steps for Recycling Right

1. Empty it: Make sure all liquid is finished before recycling.
2. Keep caps on: Plastic caps are recyclable when attached to the bottle.
3. Check local rules: Confirm what packaging your local stream accepts.
